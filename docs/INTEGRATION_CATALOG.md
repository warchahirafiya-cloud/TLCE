## Important distinctions
- Some submitted URLs are generic landing pages, not API documentation or OAuth endpoints.
- Some names (for example Aurora, Massive, Quo, Sanz, Campfire and others) are ambiguous; confirm the exact product and official integration documentation before implementation.
- A provider may not offer public API access, OAuth, or availability in the owner's region/plan.
- Legal, healthcare, life-sciences, finance, advertising, identity and recruiting systems require extra privacy, compliance and permission review.
- GPT catalogue entries are separate from SaaS integrations and cannot be treated as callable APIs.
- Do not use browser automation to accept legal terms, bypass access restrictions, or enter credentials without the owner interacting directly.

## Prioritization
**Wave 1 — platform foundation:** GitHub, Google Workspace, Dropbox, OpenAI, Railway, Netlify, Resend, Firecrawl, Exa, Tavily.
**Wave 2 — knowledge and operations:** Notion/Coda candidates, Zoom, Typeform/Tally, Semrush/Ahrefs, PostHog, Datadog.
**Wave 3 — finance and markets:** read-only market data first; accounting and exchange integrations remain read-only until individually reviewed.
**Wave 4 — regulated/high impact:** legal, healthcare, life sciences, advertising, HR/recruiting and financial transaction systems.
